# DocumentIR (v0.14.1)

DocumentIR is the immutable, Qt/Anki-independent local representation between an importer and intelligence. It contains safe IDs and labels, sections, typed blocks, bounded metadata, warnings, and source locations. Safe JSON/Markdown/plain-text renderers omit absolute paths, secrets, and long diagnostic bodies. Source locations assist review; they do not prove a claim is factually correct.
